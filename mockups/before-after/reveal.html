ZetaVote: every screen, before and after, with a new style

Second /apple-design audit, 22 Oct 2026. Drag the handle, or focus it and use the arrow keys. Left is the current build. Right is the proposed fix in the proposed style below. Desktop only, light theme. Each panel lists the guideline it comes from.

Proposed style: "Polling station"

Grounded in the real artifacts of a vote: the booth, the ballot, the counterfoil stub you keep. Calm civic surfaces everywhere, and one memorable object: the perforated stub that marks anything that is your receipt.

Only on receipt surfaces: the receipt, saved receipts, results check, and verify match. Nowhere else, so it always means "this is yours, keep it". On Vote recorded it slides in once; with reduced motion it simply appears.

Sidebar replaces the top barDesktop has width to spare. Sections sit on the left with the account at the bottom, so there is one navigation surface. sidebars.md › Best practices: no more than two levels.
Sky is spent once per screenThe primary button and the selected section only. Status uses shape plus word: a dot means open, a square means closed. branding.md: "Apply your app's accent color judiciously."
Flat, bordered cardsNo drop shadows, 10px corners, 1px #D5DCE5 borders, 8px controls at 36px tall. The logo appears once, in the sidebar. branding.md: "Ensure branding always defers to content."
