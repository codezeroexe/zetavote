ZetaVote: frosted glass proposal

A simplified Liquid Glass: a heavy frost (28px blur, 78% fill) on the floating controls only. That means the sidebar, page toolbars, menus, sheets and toasts. Cards, tables and forms stay solid white, because ballots and receipts are content. Drag each slider. Use the controls to see dark mode and the opaque fallback.

Where glass goes, and where it never goes

  • Glass: floating sidebar, page toolbar, Manage menu, sheets, toasts. Five surfaces at most per screen.
  • Solid: every card, table, form field, receipt stub and result. Votes and receipts are content.
  • Something to frost: a quiet sky and slate wash in the content layer that scrolls under the bars. The brand colour lives here, not on the bars.

liquid-glass.md › Where the material belongs: "Don't use Liquid Glass in the content layer."

Material spec

Blur, saturate28px, 1.45×
Fill light / darkwhite 78% / slate 80%
Menu / toolbar field66% (thinner)
Edge1px light rim + inner top highlight
Radiussidebar 16, toolbar 14, menu 12, sheet 18
Scroll edge6px blur band, fades out
Accent on glassprimary button only

Legibility (worst case, light)

Slate text, glass over sky11.8:1
Glass muted #46536A over sky5.9:1
Old muted #55627A over sky5.0:1
Dark: #EEF2F6 on 80% slate over white6.4:1

Fallback: prefers-reduced-transparency and prefers-contrast: more swap glass for solid card fill. Toggle above to preview.
