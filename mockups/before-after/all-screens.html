ZetaVote — all screens, before and after

Desktop only. Every "before" pane is the current build's structure as written in the source (frontend/src). Signed-in screens need an account and cannot be captured from here, so they are rebuilt from source, not screenshot. Sign-in was measured live. "After" applies the audit fixes: a single desktop header, sentence-case labels, 30px minimum controls, 28pt+ hit areas, and status shown beside the content it describes.

BeforeAfterFindings cite Apple HIG pages opened for this audit.
